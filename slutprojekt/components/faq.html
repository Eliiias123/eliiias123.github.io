Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a car wash take?

Our basic wash typically takes around 30 minutes, while our premium and ultimate packages can take up to an hour depending on the level of service you choose.

Do I need to make an appointment?

While we do accept walk-ins, we recommend making an appointment to ensure availability and reduce wait times, especially during peak hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and mobile payment options such as Apple Pay and Google Pay for your convenience.
